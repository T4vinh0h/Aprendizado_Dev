# Plural

## Singular vs Plural

- Singular: one item
- Plural: more than one

## Regular Plural Rules

- Add -s: book → books, computer → computers
- Add -es: box → boxes, watch → watches (words ending in -s, -x, -z, -ch, -sh)
- Change -y to -ies: city → cities, company → companies (consonant + y)

## Irregular Plurals

- man → men
- woman → women
- child → children
- person → people
- mouse → mice
- foot → feet
- tooth → teeth

## Examples

- One file, two files
- One class, three classes
- One company, two companies
- One person, many people
