# Sentence Structure

## Basic Components

- Subject - quem faz a ação
- Verb - a ação
- Object - quem recebe a ação
- Complement - informação adicional

## Word Order

## Main Structure: Subject + Verb + Object

### Example

I study English.

- Subject: I
- Verb: study
- Object: English

## More Examples

- She writes code.
- They build websites.
- We learn programming.
- He uses computers.
- You develop applications.

## Types of Sentences

- Affirmative: I am happy.
- Negative: I am not sad.
- Interrogative: Are you happy?
- Imperative: Study hard.
