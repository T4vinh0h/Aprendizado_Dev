# Writing

## Types of Writing

### Beginner Level

- Sentences
- Simple paragraphs
- Messages
- Short emails
- Diary entries

### Intermediate Level

- Longer paragraphs
- Stories
- Summaries
- Formal emails
- Blog posts

### Advanced Level

- Essays
- Reports
- Articles
- Academic papers
- Professional documents

## Writing Structure

### Introduction

- Hook the reader
- Present the topic
- State your thesis

### Body

- Topic sentences
- Supporting ideas
- Examples and evidence
- Logical flow

### Conclusion

- Summarize main points
- Restate thesis
- Final thoughts

## Writing Process

### 1. Planning

- Brainstorm ideas
- Organize thoughts
- Create outline

### 2. Drafting

- Write first version
- Don't worry about perfection
- Focus on ideas

### 3. Revising

- Check content
- Improve organization
- Clarify ideas

### 4. Editing

- Correct grammar
- Fix spelling
- Improve vocabulary

### 5. Proofreading

- Final check
- Look for errors
- Polish text

## Writing Tips

- Know your audience
- Use clear language
- Be concise
- Use paragraphs
- Check your work
- Read aloud
- Get feedback

## Common Structures

### Topic Sentence

- Main idea of paragraph
- Clear and specific

### Supporting Ideas

- Examples
- Explanations
- Evidence

### Transitions

- Connect ideas
- Improve flow
- Guide reader
