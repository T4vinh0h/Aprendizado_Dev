# Advanced Vocabulary

## Collocations

### Strong Collocations

- Make a decision vs Take a decision
- Do research vs Conduct research
- Take action vs Make action
- Have a discussion vs Hold a discussion

### Academic Collocations

- Draw a conclusion
- Reach a consensus
- Pose a question
- Address an issue
- Undertake research

## Synonyms and Nuance

### Knowledge

- Know - general knowledge
- Understand - comprehension
- Comprehend - deep understanding
- Grasp - quick understanding
- Realize - sudden understanding

### Think

- Think - general
- Consider - carefully
- Ponder - deeply
- Reflect - thoughtfully
- Contemplate - meditatively

### Say

- Say - general
- Tell - inform
- State - formally
- Declare - officially
- Assert - confidently

## Contextual Learning

### Word Families

- Create: create, creation, creative, creativity, creator
- Develop: develop, development, developer, developmental
- Analyze: analyze, analysis, analyst, analytical

### Register

- Formal: academic papers, business documents
- Neutral: everyday conversation, news
- Informal: friends, family, social media

## Learning Strategies

- Learn words in context
- Study word families
- Understand register differences
- Practice with authentic materials
- Use spaced repetition
- Review regularly
