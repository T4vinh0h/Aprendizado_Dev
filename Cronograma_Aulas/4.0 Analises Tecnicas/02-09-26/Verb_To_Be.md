# Verb To Be

## Present Tense

- Am (I)
- Is (He/She/It)
- Are (You/We/They)

## Past Tense

- Was (I/He/She/It)
- Were (You/We/They)

## Affirmative

- I am happy.
- She is a developer.
- They are students.

## Negative

- I am not (I'm not)
- He is not (He isn't)
- We are not (We aren't)

## Questions

- Am I late?
- Is she working?
- Are they ready?

## Contractions

- I'm
- You're
- He's
- She's
- It's
- We're
- They're
