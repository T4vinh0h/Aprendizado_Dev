# Adverbs

## Formation

Most adverbs are formed by adding -ly to adjectives:

- quick → quickly
- slow → slowly
- careful → carefully
- easy → easily
- happy → happily

## Common Adverbs

### Manner

- Quickly - rapidamente
- Slowly - lentamente
- Carefully - cuidadosamente
- Easily - facilmente
- Hardly - dificilmente

### Frequency

- Usually - geralmente
- Probably - provavelmente
- Really - realmente
- Always - sempre
- Never - nunca

### Certainty

- Probably - provavelmente
- Certainly - certamente
- Definitely - definitivamente
- Maybe - talvez

## Position in Sentence

- After the main verb: She works quickly.
- Before the adjective: It's really good.
- At the beginning: Usually, I study in the morning.

## Examples

- She writes code quickly.
- He drives carefully.
- They work hard.
- I usually arrive early.
- It's really important.
- Probably, she will come.
