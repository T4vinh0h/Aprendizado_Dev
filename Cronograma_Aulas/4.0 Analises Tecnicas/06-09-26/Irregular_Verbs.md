# Irregular Verbs

## Priority Irregular Verbs

### Most Common

- Go → Went
- Have → Had
- Do → Did
- Make → Made
- Get → Got
- See → Saw
- Take → Took
- Give → Gave
- Come → Came
- Find → Found
- Think → Thought
- Say → Said
- Know → Knew
- Feel → Felt

## More Important Irregular Verbs

- Be → Was/Were
- Become → Became
- Begin → Began
- Break → Broke
- Bring → Brought
- Buy → Bought
- Choose → Chose
- Eat → Ate
- Write → Wrote
- Read → Read
- Speak → Spoke
- Tell → Told
- Understand → Understood
- Wake → Woke
- Win → Won

## Learning Tips

- Practice with sentences
- Group similar patterns
- Use flashcards
- Review regularly

## Examples

- I went to the store yesterday.
- She had a great idea.
- He did his homework.
- They made a mistake.
- We got good news.
