# Future

## Will

### Usage

- Spontaneous decisions
- Predictions
- Promises
- Offers

### Formation

Subject + will + verb

### Examples

- I will study tomorrow.
- She will help you.
- They will arrive soon.
- We will finish the project.

## Going to

### Usage

- Plans and intentions
- Predictions based on evidence

### Formation

Subject + am/is/are + going to + verb

### Examples

- I'm going to study tomorrow.
- She is going to help you.
- They are going to arrive soon.
- We are going to finish the project.

## Key Difference

- **Will**: used for spontaneous decisions or predictions
- **Going to**: used for planned actions or intentions

### Comparison

- Will: "I will help you" (spontaneous offer)
- Going to: "I'm going to help you" (planned intention)

## Future Plans Examples

- I will call you later.
- I'm going to call you later. (already planned)
- It will rain tomorrow. (prediction)
- Look at those clouds! It's going to rain. (based on evidence)
