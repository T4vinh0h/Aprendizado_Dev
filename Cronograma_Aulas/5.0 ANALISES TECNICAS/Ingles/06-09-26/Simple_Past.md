# Simple Past

## Usage

- Completed actions in the past
- Actions that happened at a specific time

## Regular Verbs

Add -ed to the verb:

- work → worked
- study → studied
- play → played
- watch → watched
- learn → learned

## Irregular Verbs

- go → went
- have → had
- do → did
- make → made
- get → got
- see → saw
- take → took
- give → gave
- come → came
- find → found
- think → thought
- say → said
- know → knew
- feel → felt

## Affirmative

- I worked
- You worked
- He worked
- She worked
- It worked
- We worked
- They worked

## Negative

- I did not work (I didn't work)
- You did not work (You didn't work)
- He did not work (He didn't work)
- She did not work (She didn't work)
- It did not work (It didn't work)
- We did not work (We didn't work)
- They did not work (They didn't work)

## Questions

- Did I work?
- Did you work?
- Did he work?
- Did she work?
- Did it work?
- Did we work?
- Did they work?

## Examples

- I worked yesterday.
- She studied English last year.
- They visited London in 2020.
- He created a website last month.
