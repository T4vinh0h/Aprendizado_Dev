# Basic Verbs

## Essential Verbs

- Be - ser/estar
- Have - ter
- Do - fazer
- Go - ir
- Come - vir
- Get - obter/pegar
- Make - fazer/criar
- Take - pegar/tomar
- Give - dar
- Want - querer
- Need - precisar
- Like - gostar
- Love - amar
- Know - saber/conhecer
- Think - pensar
- See - ver
- Look - olhar
- Find - encontrar
- Use - usar
- Work - trabalhar
- Study - estudar
- Play - jogar/brincar
- Help - ajudar
- Try - tentar
- Ask - perguntar
- Tell - contar/dizer
- Feel - sentir

## Examples

- I have a computer.
- She works as a developer.
- They study English.
- We like programming.
- He needs help.
