# Basic Adjectives

## Size

- Big - grande
- Small - pequeno
- Large - grande
- Tiny - minúsculo
- Huge - enorme
- Little - pequeno

## Quality

- Good - bom
- Bad - mau/ruim
- Excellent - excelente
- Terrible - terrível
- Great - ótimo
- Poor - pobre/ruim

## Difficulty

- Easy - fácil
- Difficult - difícil
- Hard - difícil
- Simple - simples
- Complex - complexo

## Temperature

- Hot - quente
- Cold - frio
- Warm - morno
- Cool - fresco

## Speed

- Fast - rápido
- Slow - lento
- Quick - rápido
- Gradual - gradual

## Emotion

- Happy - feliz
- Sad - triste
- Angry - com raiva
- Calm - calmo
- Excited - animado
- Bored - entediado
- Nervous - nervoso
- Relaxed - relaxado

## Appearance

- Beautiful - bonito/bela
- Ugly - feio
- Pretty - bonito
- Handsome - bonito (para homens)
- Attractive - atraente

## Age

- Old - velho/antigo
- New - novo
- Young - jovem
- Modern - moderno
- Ancient - antigo

## Condition

- Clean - limpo
- Dirty - sujo
- Wet - molhado
- Dry - seco
- Full - cheio
- Empty - vazio

## Importance

- Important - importante
- Unimportant - sem importância
- Essential - essencial
- Necessary - necessário

## Value

- Expensive - caro
- Cheap - barato
- Valuable - valioso
- Precious - precioso

## Position

- Near - perto
- Far - longe
- Close - próximo
- Distant - distante

## Weight

- Heavy - pesado
- Light - leve

## Length

- Long - longo
- Short - curto
- Tall - alto (para pessoas)
- High - alto (para objetos)

## Color

- Red - vermelho
- Blue - azul
- Green - verde
- Yellow - amarelo
- Black - preto
- White - branco
- Orange - laranja
- Purple - roxo
- Pink - rosa
- Brown - marrom
- Gray - cinza

## Examples

### Size

- This is a big house.
- She has a small car.
- They live in a huge city.

### Quality

- It's a good day.
- That was a terrible movie.
- He is an excellent student.

### Emotion

- I am happy today.
- She feels sad.
- They are excited about the trip.

### Appearance

- She is beautiful.
- He is handsome.
- The view is pretty.

### Age

- This is an old building.
- I bought a new computer.
- He is young and energetic.

### Condition

- The room is clean.
- My shoes are dirty.
- The glass is empty.

## Position in Sentences

### Before Nouns

- A big house
- A happy person
- The red car

### After Verb "to be"

- The house is big.
- She is happy.
- The car is red.

## Comparison

### Comparative (comparando duas coisas)

- Big → Bigger
- Small → Smaller
- Good → Better
- Bad → Worse

### Superlative (o mais/menos de todos)

- Big → The biggest
- Small → The smallest
- Good → The best
- Bad → The worst

## Practice Tips

- Learn adjectives in pairs (opposites)
- Use adjectives in daily descriptions
- Practice comparatives and superlatives
- Combine adjectives (big, red car)
- Use adjectives in conversations
