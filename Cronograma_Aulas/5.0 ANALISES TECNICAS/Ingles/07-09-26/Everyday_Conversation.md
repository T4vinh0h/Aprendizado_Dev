# Everyday Conversation

## Greetings

- Hello
- Hi
- Good morning
- Good afternoon
- Good evening
- How are you?
- Nice to meet you

## Introductions

- My name is...
- I'm from...
- I live in...
- I study...
- I work with...
- I like...

## Common Questions

- How are you?
- What's your name?
- Where are you from?
- What do you do?
- How old are you?

## Common Responses

- I'm fine, thank you.
- Nice to meet you too.
- I'm a student/developer.
- I'm from Brazil.
- I'm 25 years old.

## Polite Expressions

- Please
- Thank you
- You're welcome
- Excuse me
- I'm sorry
- No problem

## Everyday Situations

### Shopping

- How much is this?
- Can I help you?
- I'm just looking.
- I'll take it.

### Restaurant

- Can I see the menu?
- I'd like to order...
- The bill, please.
- It was delicious.

### Travel

- Where is the station?
- How do I get to...?
- I need a ticket.
- What time does it leave?
