# Connectors

## Basic Connectors

### Addition

- And - e
- Also - também
- Too - também
- As well as - bem como

### Contrast

- But - mas
- However - porém/contudo
- Although - embora
- Though - embora

### Cause and Effect

- Because - porque
- So - então/por isso
- Therefore - portanto
- Consequently - consequentemente

### Sequence

- Then - então/depois
- First - primeiro
- Finally - finalmente
- Next - próximo
- Last - último

## Examples

### Addition

- I like coffee and tea.
- She studies English. Also, she learns Spanish.
- He works hard. He plays hard too.

### Contrast

- I studied hard, but I didn't pass.
- It was raining. However, we went out.
- Although it was late, she called me.

### Cause and Effect

- I studied because I wanted to learn.
- It was raining, so we stayed home.
- He was tired, therefore he went to sleep.

### Sequence

- First, read the instructions. Then, start working.
- Finally, check your work.
- Next, add the code.
