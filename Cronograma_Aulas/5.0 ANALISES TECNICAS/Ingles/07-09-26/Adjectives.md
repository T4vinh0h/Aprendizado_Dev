# Adjectives

## Basic Adjectives

### Size

- Big / Small
- Large / Tiny
- Huge / Little

### Quality

- Good / Bad
- Easy / Difficult
- Beautiful / Ugly

### Temperature

- Hot / Cold
- Warm / Cool

### Speed

- Fast / Slow
- Quick / Gradual

### Emotion

- Happy / Sad
- Angry / Calm
- Excited / Bored

### Age

- Old / New
- Young / Ancient

### Difficulty

- Easy / Hard
- Simple / Complex

## Comparative

### Formation

- Short adjectives: add -er
  - big → bigger
  - fast → faster
  - small → smaller

- Long adjectives: use "more"
  - beautiful → more beautiful
  - difficult → more difficult
  - important → more important

- Irregular
  - good → better
  - bad → worse
  - far → farther/further

## Superlative

### Formation

- Short adjectives: add -est
  - big → the biggest
  - fast → the fastest
  - small → the smallest

- Long adjectives: use "most"
  - beautiful → the most beautiful
  - difficult → the most difficult
  - important → the most important

- Irregular
  - good → the best
  - bad → the worst
  - far → the farthest/furthest

## Examples

- This computer is fast.
- This computer is faster than that one.
- This is the fastest computer.
- She is happy.
- She is happier today.
- She is the happiest person I know.
