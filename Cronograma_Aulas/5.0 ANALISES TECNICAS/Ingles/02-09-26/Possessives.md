# Possessives

## Possessive Adjectives

- My
- Your
- His
- Her
- Its
- Our
- Their

## Possessive Pronouns

- Mine
- Yours
- Hers
- Ours
- Theirs

## Examples

- This is my computer.
- That is your code.
- His project is finished.
- Her website is beautiful.
- Its function works well.
- Our team is great.
- Their application is popular.

- This book is mine.
- That phone is yours.
- The success is hers.
- The victory is ours.
- The idea is theirs.
