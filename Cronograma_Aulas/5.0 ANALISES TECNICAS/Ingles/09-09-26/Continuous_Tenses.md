# Continuous Tenses

## Present Continuous

Subject + am/is/are + verb + -ing

- I am working now.
- She is studying at the moment.
- They are developing a project.

## Past Continuous

Subject + was/were + verb + -ing

### Usage

- Actions in progress at a specific time in the past
- Interrupted actions
- Background description

### Examples

- I was working when you called.
- She was studying at 8 PM yesterday.
- They were sleeping when the alarm rang.

## Present Perfect Continuous

Subject + have/has + been + verb + -ing

### Usage

- Actions started in the past and continuing now
- Actions with duration emphasis
- Recent activities with present results

### Examples

- I have been working here for 5 years.
- She has been studying English since 2020.
- They have been developing this project for months.

## Comparison

### Present Continuous vs Past Continuous

- Present: happening now
- Past: was happening at a specific past time

### Present Perfect vs Present Perfect Continuous

- Present Perfect: focus on result
- Present Perfect Continuous: focus on duration/activity

## Examples

- I work here. (general fact)
- I am working here now. (happening now)
- I was working here yesterday. (was happening)
- I have worked here for 5 years. (experience)
- I have been working here for 5 years. (emphasizing duration)
