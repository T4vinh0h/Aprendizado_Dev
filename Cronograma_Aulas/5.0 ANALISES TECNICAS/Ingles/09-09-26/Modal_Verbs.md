# Modal Verbs

## Characteristics

- No -s in third person singular
- Followed by base form of verb
- No infinitive form
- No past participle

## Can / Could

### Can

- Ability: I can swim.
- Permission: Can I go?
- Possibility: It can happen.

### Could

- Past ability: I could swim when I was young.
- Polite request: Could you help me?
- Possibility: It could happen.

## May / Might

### May

- Permission: May I come in?
- Possibility: It may rain today.

### Might

- Possibility (less certain): It might rain today.

## Must

- Obligation: I must study.
- Necessity: You must wear a seatbelt.
- Strong deduction: She must be tired.

## Should

- Advice: You should study more.
- Recommendation: I should visit the doctor.
- Expectation: It should work.

## Would

- Polite requests: Would you help me?
- Preferences: I would like coffee.
- Conditionals: I would go if I could.

## Examples

- I can speak English.
- Could you open the door?
- It may rain tomorrow.
- You must be careful.
- She should rest.
- I would like to learn programming.

## Common Patterns

- Can you help me? (request)
- I must go now. (obligation)
- You should try this. (advice)
- It might work. (possibility)
