## Shadowing Technique

### Steps

1. **Listen** - Listen to native speaker
2. **Pause** - Stop the audio
3. **Repeat** - Repeat exactly what you heard
4. **Imitate** - Copy the pronunciation, rhythm, and intonation

### Benefits

- Improves pronunciation
- Develops natural rhythm
- Enhances intonation
- Builds muscle memory

### Practice Routine

- Start with short phrases
- Use slow audio initially
- Gradually increase speed
- Record yourself
- Compare with original
