# Phrasal Verbs

## What are Phrasal Verbs?

Phrasal verbs are verbs combined with particles (prepositions or adverbs) that create a new meaning.

## Common Phrasal Verbs

### Get

- Get up - levantar
- Get ready - preparar-se
- Get along - dar-se bem
- Get over - superar

### Wake

- Wake up - acordar

### Go

- Go out - sair
- Go on - continuar
- Go back - voltar

### Come

- Come back - voltar
- Come in - entrar
- Come on - vamos/depressa

### Look

- Look for - procurar
- Look at - olhar para
- Look after - cuidar de
- Look forward to - aguardar com ansiedade

### Find

- Find out - descobrir

### Give

- Give up - desistir
- Give away - dar/entregar
- Give back - devolver

### Turn

- Turn on - ligar
- Turn off - desligar
- Turn up - aumentar volume/chegar
- Turn down - diminuir volume/recusar

### Pick

- Pick up - pegar/buscar

### Put

- Put on - vestir
- Put off - adiar
- Put away - guardar

### Take

- Take off - tirar/decolar
- Take away - levar embora

### Figure

- Figure out - descobrir/entender

## Examples

- I get up at 7 AM every day.
- She is looking for a new job.
- They gave up on the project.
- Can you turn on the lights?
- I need to figure out this problem.
- He picked up the phone.
- She took off her coat.

## Learning Tips

- Learn in context
- Group by particle
- Practice with examples
- Use in conversation
