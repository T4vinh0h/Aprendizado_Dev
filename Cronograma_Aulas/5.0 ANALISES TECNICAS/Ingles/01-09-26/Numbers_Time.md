## Dates

### Formatos

- **American:** Month/Day/Year (MM/DD/YYYY)
  - 09/16/2024 - September 16, 2024

- **British:** Day/Month/Year (DD/MM/YYYY)
  - 16/09/2024 - 16 September 2024

### Writing Dates

- September 16, 2024
- 16th September 2024
- The 16th of September, 2024

## Time

### Telling Time

- **O'clock:** exato
  - It's 8:00 (eight o'clock)

- **Half past:** meia hora após
  - It's 8:30 (half past eight)

- **Quarter past:** quinze minutos após
  - It's 8:15 (quarter past eight)

- **Quarter to:** quinze minutos antes
  - It's 8:45 (quarter to nine)

### Digital Time

- 8:00 AM - eight in the morning
- 12:00 PM - noon/midday
- 8:00 PM - eight in the evening
- 12:00 AM - midnight

### Time Expressions

- In the morning - de manhã
- In the afternoon - à tarde
- In the evening - à noite
- At night - de noite
- At noon - ao meio-dia
- At midnight - à meia-noite
