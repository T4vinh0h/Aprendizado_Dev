# Adverbs of Frequency

## Common Adverbs

- Always - sempre (100%)
- Usually - geralmente (80-90%)
- Often - frequentemente (60-70%)
- Sometimes - às vezes (50%)
- Rarely - raramente (10-20%)
- Never - nunca (0%)

## Position in Sentence

Before the main verb:

- I always study English.
- She usually works from home.
- He often visits the library.
- We sometimes eat at restaurants.
- They rarely miss classes.
- You never give up.

After the verb "to be":

- I am always happy.
- She is usually busy.
- He is often late.
- We are sometimes tired.
- They are rarely absent.
- You are never wrong.

## Examples

- I always drink coffee in the morning.
- She usually arrives on time.
- He often checks his email.
- We sometimes work on weekends.
- They rarely make mistakes.
- I never forget my keys.
