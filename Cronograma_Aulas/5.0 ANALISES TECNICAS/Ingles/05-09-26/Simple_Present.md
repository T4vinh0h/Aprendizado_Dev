# Simple Present

## Usage

- Habits and routines
- General truths
- Permanent situations

## Affirmative

- I work
- You work
- He works
- She works
- It works
- We work
- They work

## Negative

- I do not work (I don't work)
- You do not work (You don't work)
- He does not work (He doesn't work)
- She does not work (She doesn't work)
- It does not work (It doesn't work)
- We do not work (We don't work)
- They do not work (They don't work)

## Questions

- Do I work?
- Do you work?
- Does he work?
- Does she work?
- Does it work?
- Do we work?
- Do they work?

## Third Person Rule

Add -s to the verb in third person singular (he, she, it):

- I study → He studies
- You go → She goes
- We work → It works

## Examples

- I work as a developer.
- She studies programming.
- They use computers daily.
- He writes code every morning.
