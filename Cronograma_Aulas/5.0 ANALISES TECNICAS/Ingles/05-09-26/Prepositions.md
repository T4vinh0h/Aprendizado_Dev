# Prepositions

## Basic Prepositions

- In - em/dentro
- On - em/sobre
- At - em/a
- To - para
- From - de
- For - para/por
- With - com
- Without - sem
- About - sobre
- Before - antes de
- After - depois de
- Between - entre
- Under - embaixo
- Over - sobre/acima
- Behind - atrás
- Next to - ao lado de
- Near - perto

## Prepositions of Time

- In 2026
- In the morning
- In July
- On Monday
- On my birthday
- At 8 PM
- At noon
- At night

## Prepositions of Place

- In the room
- On the table
- At the office
- Under the bed
- Behind the door
- Between the chairs
- Next to the window
- Near the school

## Examples

- I work in an office.
- The book is on the table.
- We meet at 9 AM.
- I go to school every day.
- She comes from Brazil.
- This is for you.
- I work with my team.
- I can't work without my computer.
