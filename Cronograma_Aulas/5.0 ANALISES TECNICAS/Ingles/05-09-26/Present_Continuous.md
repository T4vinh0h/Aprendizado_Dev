# Present Continuous

## Usage

- Actions happening now
- Temporary situations
- Future arrangements

## Formation

Subject + am/is/are + verb + -ing

## Affirmative

- I am studying (I'm studying)
- You are working (You're working)
- He is coding (He's coding)
- She is learning (She's learning)
- It is running (It's running)
- We are developing (We're developing)
- They are testing (They're testing)

## Negative

- I am not studying (I'm not studying)
- You are not working (You aren't working)
- He is not coding (He isn't coding)
- She is not learning (She isn't learning)
- It is not running (It isn't running)
- We are not developing (We aren't developing)
- They are not testing (They aren't testing)

## Questions

- Am I studying?
- Are you working?
- Is he coding?
- Is she learning?
- Is it running?
- Are we developing?
- Are they testing?

## Examples

- I am reading a book now.
- She is writing code at the moment.
- They are working on a project today.
- We are learning English this semester.
