# Questions

## Yes/No Questions

Questions that can be answered with yes or no.

- Are you a student?
- Do you like programming?
- Is she working?
- Can you help me?

## Wh- Questions

Questions that ask for specific information.

### What

- What is your name?
- What do you do?

### Where

- Where do you live?
- Where is the office?

### When

- When do you study?
- When is the meeting?

### Who

- Who is your teacher?
- Who works here?

### Why

- Why are you learning English?
- Why did you choose this course?

### Which

- Which computer do you use?
- Which language do you prefer?

### How

- How do you learn?
- How old are you?

## Question Formation

- Use auxiliary verbs (do, does, did, is, are, can)
- Invert subject and verb
